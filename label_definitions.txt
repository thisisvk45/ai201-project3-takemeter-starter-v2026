# My label definitions, one per line, as `label: definition`.
# Copied from the README so the baseline is judged on exactly what I wrote.
evidence: backs its point with at least one specific thing a reader could check, such as a number, a link, a quote, a version, or how a product or system actually behaves
experience: backs its point with something that happened to the writer or their team, first hand
opinion: a claim, judgment, joke, or line of reasoning with no checkable specific and no first hand experience behind it
question: the main purpose is to ask for information the writer does not have and wants answered
